# **What is a Chit Fund? — Simple Explanation**

A chit fund is a trusted group savings scheme. A fixed number of members come together and each contributes an amount every month. Every month, one member receives the entire pooled amount — either through an auction (for those who need funds urgently) or by turn. This continues until every member has received their payout once.

It is a simple, powerful way to save money and access a lump sum exactly when you need it — without complicated bank paperwork or long waiting periods.

# **How It Works — Step by Step**

* Join a Scheme — Choose a chit value that suits your budget and register with us.

* Pay Monthly — Each month, your installment is the chit value divided by the number of members, minus the kazar (dividend) shared among all members from that month’s auction. The higher the auction bid, the more dividend you earn, and the lower your actual payment that month.

* Monthly Auction — Every month, an auction is held. Members who need funds urgently can bid for that month’s payout. The highest bidder receives the chit amount after deduction of the commission.

* Receive Your Payout — Either win the auction when you need the money, or wait patiently — you will receive your payment amount at the end of the tenure.

* Complete the Tenure — Every member receives their payout exactly once over the duration of the scheme.
